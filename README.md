# Restaurant Feasibility Toolkit

**Open templates and frameworks for assessing whether a restaurant concept is worth opening — before you sign a lease.**

I built these tools while developing my own restaurant venture in Paris (2025–2026): market and competitive research, concept design, financial modelling, on-the-ground feasibility work, and pitching to private investors. This repo is the anonymised, reusable version of that process — the method and the structure, with example figures only.

> All numbers in this toolkit are **illustrative examples**, not real data from my project. Ratios and regulatory references are based on the **French market** (VAT rates, lease types, licences) but the frameworks themselves are country-agnostic.

## What's inside

| File | What it does |
|------|--------------|
| [`01-market-study.md`](01-market-study.md) | Framework for a full market & competition study: segments, trends, 15-competitor scoring grid, district-by-district analysis, pricing benchmark, SWOT |
| [`02-concept-positioning.md`](02-concept-positioning.md) | Canvas for defining and stress-testing a restaurant concept: target customer, positioning map, menu architecture, differentiation |
| [`03-financial-model.xlsx`](03-financial-model.xlsx) | Full financial template with live formulas: revenue build per daypart with weekday patterns and ramp-up, 4-year P&L, investment plan & working capital, loan amortisation with deferral, break-even at 4 rent scenarios, KPI dashboard |
| [`04-opening-checklist.md`](04-opening-checklist.md) | On-the-ground feasibility: location scoring grid, lease essentials, licences & permits, suppliers, funding structure, T-9 months timeline |
| [`05-investor-deck-outline.md`](05-investor-deck-outline.md) | The slide-by-slide structure of a restaurant investor deck: concept → market proof → execution → business plan → ask |

## Who this is for

- First-time restaurant founders who want structure before spending money
- Students or analysts working on hospitality / F&B business cases
- Investors who want a quick grid to sanity-check a restaurant pitch

## How to use it

1. Start with `01-market-study.md` — if the market doesn't work, nothing else matters.
2. Define the concept with `02-concept-positioning.md`.
3. Plug your own hypotheses into `03-financial-model.xlsx` (blue cells = inputs, everything else calculates itself).
4. Use `04-opening-checklist.md` to confront the plan with reality: location, lease, licences, suppliers, funding.
5. When the numbers hold, assemble the pitch with `05-investor-deck-outline.md`.

## The one rule I learned

**Fall in love with the problem, not with your concept.** Every template here exists to force a kill-decision as early and as cheaply as possible. A feasibility study that can't say "no" is just decoration for a decision already made.
